# AI citation diagnosis and improvement

Use with keyword-cycle.md sections 6–9 when the requested outcome concerns
AI Overview or AI Mode citations. Follow the project's objective order: if AI
citations come first, select work against that outcome while maintaining search
eligibility and preserving useful natural rankings. Do not turn this into an
unrequested full-site rewrite or abandon independent keyword discovery.

## 1. Establish the exact-query baseline

Resume prior observations, protected passages, pending releases and saved-crawl
checks before selecting changes. Retain exact query, engine/surface, country,
language, device, actual snapshot/report date and retrieval time. Preserve source
and account timezone uncertainty. Nearby variants require their own observations.
Prefer authorized cached reports; new searches remain subject to exposure policy.

For each query record separate states:

| Evidence | Explicit states / boundary |
|---|---|
| AI answer | present, absent_in_snapshot, blocked_or_unknown |
| Source-list coverage | complete_for_snapshot, partial, unavailable |
| Own linked citation | observed, not_observed_in_complete_snapshot, unknown |
| Own brand mention | observed or not_observed within the actual read; a mention alone is not a linked citation |
| Citation order | observed order with the UI surface identified, or unknown; card order is not a guaranteed stable ranking |
| Natural result | actual position and URL if returned, otherwise unknown/not_returned; separate from any mixed tracker position |

When visible cards omit the site but the expanded list is unreadable, own citation
presence remains unknown. AIO absent does not mean a citation loss or a content
failure. A prior cached citation remains historical, not proof of current presence.
Record exact linked URLs, displayed answer claims, source cards and list scope.
A source may support one claim without supporting the entire AI answer. Never
claim a full citation audit from the first cards or a tracker feature icon.

For a loss, compare compatible prior/current exact-query snapshots: did the answer
stop triggering, sources rotate, the cited own URL switch, the natural owner
change, or the source page version change? Report what is established and what
remains unexplained. A different monitor denominator alone neither proves nor
dismisses a material same-query decline.

For a material cohort, save a dated baseline while the provider report is
accessible. Keep exact member rows, scope, observed positions and URLs, and
per-query visibility when the provider exposes it. Retain the visible AI
excerpt and source URLs for priority queries. If a project is replaced, match
exact rows at the same scope and keep the project histories separate. Today's
contribution alone cannot explain a portfolio change when prior rows are missing.

## 2. Map answer needs to actual source passages

Read the cited competitors' actual pages and the site's actual participating URLs,
not merely their titles, intended owners or tool suggestions. Map each important
answer claim/subquestion to the supporting passage, evidence type and date. Distinguish
native setup, buying/comparison, human service, operational support and adjacent
markets. Do not force a commercial page into an answer seeking native instructions.

For each material gap retain, in the existing per-query/job record:

- the answer need and actual competing source URL/passage;
- the corresponding own section and whether it already answers that need;
- live and search-saved version evidence, including intended body markers;
- the specific missing answer, proof, qualification, internal link or source access;
- an existing-page revision, distinct supporting job, watch or reject decision,
  with rationale, evidence limits and next check.

Classify the gap: content/intent, saved-version or eligibility, product capability,
proof, or independent authority/distribution. Copy cannot repair missing capability
or create independent corroboration. Unknown saved content is an evidence gap,
not a demonstrated rendering defect. A wrong-looking URL can be a legitimate
answer owner: establish its intent and competing pages before changing ownership.

## 3. Make the useful claim easy to verify

Prefer replacing the weakest relevant passage over adding repeated modules.
Give a direct answer with an explicit subject, scope, conditions and exceptions;
keep supporting evidence and useful source links close to the claim. Tables,
examples or diagrams are optional when they make a comparison or task clearer.
No fixed word count, heading formula, FAQ quantity or keyword density is required.

Read the proposed answer block by itself before editing it into the page. A
paragraph, comparison row or procedure should still identify the product or
option, the job it solves, and any condition that changes the answer. Keep plan,
channel, permission and human approval limits next to the claim they qualify.

Describe verified combinations and control boundaries precisely: compatibility
with incumbent tools, what remains under user control, workflow-specific modes,
translation, task follow-through and execution limits where relevant to the job.
These are examples to investigate, not assumed capabilities of every product.
Use a dated competitor matrix to distinguish parity, proven advantage, gap and
unknown. A useful combination may differentiate the offer without proving that
no competitor has it. Select the capabilities needed for this answer rather than
listing every feature on every page.

Use original observations, reproducible procedures or consented/redacted records
with provenance. Separate configured capability, proposed action, approval,
provider receipt and real-world completion. A correction-to-later-reuse narrative
requires linked evidence of both events; neither a screenshot nor a product
capability claim invents that continuity. Label illustrative examples as examples.
If proof is missing, narrow the assertion or retain an evidence task.

Link from relevant tutorials/comparisons to the commercial owner and from claims
to useful proof/help where appropriate. Avoid exact-anchor stuffing, blanket
sitewide links, duplicate acquisition pages and unsupported superlatives.

## 4. Learn from citations without destroying them

Compare observed citation winners with stable/non-cited controls of similar intent,
using their exact queries, actual source passages, saved versions and timelines.
Extract bounded hypotheses such as a clearer category distinction, a useful
comparison or verifiable original evidence. Do not claim that one quoted-looking
sentence caused selection, or transplant a successful template into every intent.

Check the accuracy of the displayed AI claim as well as the linked citation.
Record the answer wording, the card excerpt if shown, and the matching passage.
Label the passage as directly observed in the card, inferred from the source
page, or unknown. Confirm material claims such as pricing, channel support,
permissions and automatic execution against the current authoritative source.
If the AI wording overgeneralizes a supported claim, record the mismatch and
fix a verified ambiguity at its source. A correct page can still be summarized
inaccurately, so preserve a clear passage unless evidence supports a change.

Record the currently cited URL and passage as a protected baseline. Preserve its
useful meaning, proof and links; change it only for factual defects or a supported
new need, with explicit rationale and a recoverable diff. Protection is not a
permanent freeze. Keep a local edit distinct from a deployed/saved version, and
never repeatedly rewrite content whose newest authorized release has not yet
been evaluated unless a new concrete defect justifies it.

## 5. Evaluate citations as a separate outcome

Use a stable, named query cohort and compatible snapshot scopes. Report counts
with denominators: queries checked/selected; AIO present/observable queries;
complete/partial source lists; linked own citations/complete AIO-positive snapshots;
and own citations in partial lists separately. Unknowns are not negative results.
Report per-query transitions alongside totals so a strong head term is not hidden
by many weak additions. Keep explicitly observed brand mentions separate.

For repeated comparable checks, report query-level persistence and source-URL
changes with dates/sample counts; one appearance is not stable coverage. A higher
blue-link position does not establish a citation gain. Aggregate/page AI impressions,
exact-query citations, natural ranking, visits and qualified business outcomes
remain distinct; do not add overlapping provider metrics.

Require the production version and intended saved passage before attributing a
post-change reading to new copy. Retain competing explanations and controls;
observational improvement is association, not proof of causation. Record retain,
revise, rollback_recommended or inconclusive with the next action/date in the
existing ledger and unified report. An index request is not citation success.

## Platform boundary

[Google's AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)
states that supporting pages need indexing and snippet eligibility; there is no
special AI schema or required AI text file, and inclusion is not guaranteed.
Related-search fan-out motivates useful subquestion coverage, not fabricated
long-tail demand or pages for every wording. Recheck primary documentation when
platform claims change. This workflow is an evidence-led editorial hypothesis,
not a hidden ranking recipe or permission to publish, index, spend or reach out.
