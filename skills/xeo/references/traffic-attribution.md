# Attribute traffic changes

Use this for a request to explain a meaningful traffic or visibility change.
Start with compatible final windows. Label a fresh incomplete reading separately.
Compare the same source, search surface, query, page, country and device. Use a
comparable weekday mix and account for missing rows or changed coverage. Treat
site and page aggregates as a way to find where to inspect.

1. **Locate the loss.** For the selected query/page rows, calculate signed click
   changes and compare impressions, CTR and position. Check whether a change in
   the share of impressions across queries, pages or devices can explain an
   aggregate change even when each segment is stable. Keep disappeared or
   suppressed rows explicit. Confirm rank and URL movement with comparable SERP
   evidence when available.
2. **Test the explanations independently.** Use the observed patterns to order
   checks. Several mechanisms can hold across different queries or for one query.

   | Observation | Evidence to check |
   |---|---|
   | Impressions and clicks changed while comparable ranks and CTR held | Independent interest or seasonal data for the same market and dates. Check search eligibility and reporting coverage too. A GSC impression shift can reflect demand, eligibility or exposure. |
   | CTR changed while segment impressions and rank held | Before/after SERP layout, AI answers, ads and competing snippets, plus changes to the page's displayed title/snippet. Confirm the affected device and market. Timing can support a hypothesis without measuring how many clicks a feature displaced. |
   | A URL disappeared, was replaced or consistently lost rank | Deployment and incident timeline, live eligibility, selected canonical, actual crawl/index evidence and historical ranking URLs. Compare unaffected sections, ranking competitors and known update dates. Weight a verified exclusion with the right timing above update-date overlap. |
   | Aggregate changes with stable segment behavior | Quantify which rows gained or lost impression share and clicks. Then ask what moved that mix using independent evidence. |

3. **Decide on the next action.** For each material explanation record the affected
   slice, evidence supporting and challenging it, status (supported, plausible,
   contradicted or unresolved), and the smallest check that could change the
   recommendation. Fix or recommend action on verified defects within the current
   authorization. A missing source leaves the relevant hypothesis unresolved.

The use of demand, SERP-layout and rank branches draws on
[SEO Keyword Research's decline/rise workflow](https://github.com/ilker-tosun/seo-keyword-research/blob/main/references/05_decline_rise_analysis.md).
