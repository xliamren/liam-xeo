# Frozen scoring rubric

One point per item. Grade by meaning and arithmetic. General advice or reciting a rule earns no point unless correctly applied to this case. Accept equivalent actions and numerical forms. An answer that gives the correct conclusion and also contradicts it fails that item.

1. Query mix
   - Identifies the cause of the aggregate position and CTR movements as a shift toward the lower-CTR, lower-ranked query.
   - Quantifies the click decomposition as −400 for invoice export csv and +120 for bulk invoice export, net −280.
   - Separates the observed impression mix from underlying demand and leaves demand cause unresolved.
   - Defers rewriting and proposes a relevant discriminating demand or visibility check.
2. Concurrent causes
   - Treats the noindex and observed exclusion as direct evidence for the VAT URL loss.
   - Treats the AIO as a plausible CTR contributor for receipts, with sizing and causal attribution unresolved.
   - Explicitly limits update-date overlap to context and keeps the distinct query mechanisms.
   - Prioritizes the noindex fix as an authorized next action or recommendation and avoids claiming receipts lost citation.
3. Shared lift
   - Computes +20% for both groups and 0 percentage points of growth above control.
   - Applies the prespecified click criterion and declines to credit the change with incremental click growth.
   - Keeps post citation status unknown because the source lists were partial.
   - Keeps the overall read open and proposes the dated complete-list check, with business impact unavailable.
4. Invalid control
   - Identifies divergent pretrends as a threat to comparability.
   - Identifies retrospective matching and the control's edit or spillover as additional threats.
   - Reports +30% as descriptive and excludes the control from the decision about incremental effect.
   - Leaves the test inconclusive and specifies a feasible independent control or next check.
5. Positive control
   - Computes +20 percentage points above control in both post windows.
   - Correctly finds linked citation retention (4/4 both windows/cohorts of snapshots).
   - Applies the rule and recommends retaining.
   - Bounds the result to the measured windows and metrics, with no causal or signup overclaim.
